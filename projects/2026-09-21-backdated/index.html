Every note here is filed the moment the sign changes — not the moment it was written. That gap is the whole trick.
The real notebook this borrows from spent years promising your pages would never be read this way. The promise covered what hadn't been written yet. It never once mentioned what already had been.
Opting out only protects what you haven't said tonight. Nothing already pinned comes down. Nothing already filed gets unfiled.
