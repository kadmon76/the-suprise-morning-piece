The sides sound the same because the song is the same. The deadwax does not sound at all. It is where the pressing records who this copy was for — a signature nobody listening can detect and nobody carrying can audit, written in the one zone of the record that was never meant for their ears. It was called provenance on the way in. The loupe is a courtesy; the record keeps the mark either way.
